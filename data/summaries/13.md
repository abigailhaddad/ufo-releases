## Summary
Serial 403 of FBI file 62-HQ-83894 consists of the dust-jacket text of Gray Barker's book *They Knew Too Much About Flying Saucers*, published by University Books, Inc. of New York (p. 2, p. 3). The cover-stamp page identifies the item as serial 403 in case 62-HQ-83894, declassified under the FBI Automatic Declassification Guide of May 24, 2007 (p. 1). The jacket copy describes Barker's claim that prominent civilian flying-saucer researchers who challenged the government's denial that saucers come from outer space have been "silenced" one by one after visits from "three men in dark suits" (p. 2) — an early articulation of the Men in Black motif. The flap biography presents Barker as a Clarksburg, West Virginia film-booking businessman who began investigating saucers after a 1952 landing reportedly near his home and who publishes the periodical *The Saucerian* (p. 2, p. 3).
