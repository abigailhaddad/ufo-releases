## Summary
Section 10 opens with an 8/31/1966 letter from Mrs. Florence C. Dow of Goffstown, NH, a NICAP member who suspected the Amalgamated Flying Saucer Clubs of America (AFSCA) magazine was Communist-backed and asked Hoover to clear her name (pp. 4–5); Hoover replied that the Bureau neither evaluates organizations nor has any record of AFSCA (p. 2). The bulk is the enclosed July 1966 "Flying Saucers International," edited by Gabriel Green, featuring a transcribed message from "Master Kalen-Li Retan" of the planet Korendor — received via shortwave by contactee Bob Renaud — denouncing the U.S. "Military-Industrial complex" and the Vietnam War (p. 9). It also contains the AFSCA 3rd National Convention program (Centennial Coliseum, Reno, July 8–10, 1966) and reprinted clippings on early-1966 sightings across North America (pp. 8, 11–15). FBI memos close the section: an LA SAC report finding the Korendor article "expounded the Communist Party (CP) line" but recommending no action (p. 18), and an SF SAC summary noting the Air Force's $300,000 contract with Dr. Edward U. Condon at the University of Colorado for an 18-month UFO study (pp. 25, 29).

## Key facts
- Hoover's 9/6/1966 reply to Mrs. Dow declines to evaluate AFSCA (p. 2).
- AFSCA's 3rd National Convention: July 8–10, 1966, Centennial Coliseum, Reno, NV; 21 listed speakers including Gabriel Green, George Van Tassel, Dr. Daniel W. Fry, Dr. Frank E. Stranges, Howard Menger, and Mel Noel (pp. 8, 12).
- "Korendor" message attributed to Bob Renaud's 5/4/1966 shortwave reception claims Kennedy was killed for opposing the Military-Industrial complex (p. 9).
- LA SAC memo (10/3/1966) forwards the magazine via Philadelphia complainant Jarvis H. Cooper (IRS); no investigation contemplated (p. 18).
- SF SAC memo (10/19/1966) reports Rep. Gerald Ford urging a Congressional UFO probe and the Condon commission (p. 25).
- Clipping: 87 Hillsdale, MI coeds and CD director William Van Horn report a hovering object 3/20/1966; Dr. J. Allen Hynek attributes it to swamp gas (pp. 14–15).
- Clipping: Ravenna, OH deputies chase a UFO 85 miles into Pennsylvania; Mantua Police Chief Gerald Buchert photographs it but is told by the Air Force not to release the image (p. 11).
- Air Force tally cited: 10,147 sightings since 1947, 646 unexplained (p. 30).
