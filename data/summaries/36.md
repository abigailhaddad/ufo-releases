# DOW-UAP-D12, Mission Report, Iraq, May 2022

**Record:** 36 · **Agency:** Department of War · **Incident:** 5/20/22, Iraq
**Source:** `data/text/36.txt` · **Pages:** 6

## Summary
This Mission Report (MISREP) documents an 18-hour 54-minute Air Force reconnaissance sortie flown out of OKAS on 20 May 2022 under Operation INHERENT RESOLVE, tasked by USCENTCOM through the 609th AOC and originated by the 196 ATKS (p. 1, p. 2, p. 4). The aircraft collected SIGINT via AIRHANDLER Version 2 from 0614Z to 2346Z and supported OP PHANTOM FLEX in support of MAG, with FMV exploited by DGS 1 (p. 1, p. 5). At 2043Z, while at 18,000 ft, the crew observed a single unidentified aerial phenomenon flying north to northeast and followed it as long as possible (p. 6). The screener could not obtain a positive identification of the UAP, and its altitude, velocity, and trajectory were logged as unknown (p. 6). The report was declassified on 8 October 2025 by MG Richard A. Harrison, USCENTCOM Chief of Staff, at SECRET//REL TO USA, FVEY (p. 1).

## Key facts
- Takeoff at 200542Z MAY22 from OKAS, delayed due to comm issues; landing at 210036Z MAY22, total mission time 18 hours 54 minutes (p. 4).
- Mission was a planned RECONNAISSANCE tasking under ATO "AP," supporting MAG with 136 minutes of precoordination rated SATISFACTORY (p. 2, p. 5).
- ISR window ran 200927Z to 202121Z, conducting FMV and SI in killbox 89AS (p. 4, p. 5).
- Initial UAP contact logged at 202043Z MAY22 with friendly aircraft at 18,000 ft; one UAP sighted (p. 6).
- UAP MDS/asset type and tail number recorded as UNK; physical state, signatures, and RF data left blank or "-" (p. 6).
- Equipment fit included ANDAS4 targeting pod and AHv2 additional avionics (p. 3).
- Weather "was not a factor"; intel gap was reported as filled and the asset assessed effective (p. 5).
- Declassification date originally set for 20470521; redactions cite 1.4a, 1.4c, 1.4g and FOIA (b)(3), (b)(6), 130b (p. 1, p. 2).
