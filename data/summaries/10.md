## Summary
This serial from FBI HQ file 62-HQ-83894 (Vol. 1, Serial 153) is a Knoxville Field Office submission concerning "Flying Saucers" observed over the Oak Ridge, Tennessee area, classified under "Internal Security - X" (p. 2). The package transmits two photographs reputedly showing flying saucers seen at Oak Ridge during July 1947, along with a photostatic copy of a Knoxville News-Sentinel clipping about the sightings (p. 2). The newspaper account credits W. R. Presley of 218 Illinois Avenue, Oak Ridge, who used a leftover frame on a family roll to photograph a nearby mountain and unexpectedly captured what the paper calls a "Flying Saucer" (p. 4). The article notes this is reportedly the first time such an object had been photographed over Oak Ridge and says the picture "has all of Oak Ridge talking" (p. 4). The record was processed under FBI Automatic Declassification Guide authority issued May 24, 2007 (p. 1).
