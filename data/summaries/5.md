## Summary
Section 5 of FBI file 62-HQ-83894 (serials 186-245) collects mid-1949 correspondence on flying-disc reports. A San Antonio SAC memo notifies Hoover that an Alexandria, Louisiana club planned a national convention for flying-saucer witnesses, advertised in the Galveston News on July 10, 1949 (p. 3). The bulk traces Walter Winchell's intermediary Ernest Cuneo passing the Bureau a letter from Peter Camerlon Jones of Los Angeles, who claimed that in August 1947 he saw a silver-greenish top-shaped metal object with two windows while hiking, and speculated about an "inter-global landing" reacting to atomic tests via "negative gravity" propulsion (p. 21). Hoover ordered L.A. SAC R. B. Hood to investigate, but Jones could not be located and the Bureau concluded the letter "may have been a prank" (p. 9, p. 15, p. 24). The section closes with an ONI Confidential report (Serial 38-49, 23 June 1949) on 6-7 elongated-oval unpainted-metal discs sighted in file formation over Hart Mountain, Oregon on 27 May 1949 by LTJG Joseph C. Shell, USNR (p. 26, p. 28).

## Key facts
- An Alexandria, Louisiana club planned a national flying-saucer-witness convention, advertised July 10, 1949 (p. 3).
- Cuneo relayed Jones's August 1947 sighting letter to Hoover and Ladd for Winchell (p. 5, p. 21).
- Jones described a silver-green top-shaped craft with apparent windows that knocked him down when it took off silently (p. 21).
- Jones theorized the craft might be an extraterrestrial probe reacting to atomic detonations, possibly using "negative gravity" (p. 21).
- Hoover ordered L.A. to check Jones's background and interview him (p. 10, p. 17).
- SAC Hood reported Jones could not be located at 164 W. 37th St. (p. 15, p. 24).
- The Bureau wrote Cuneo on July 21, 1949 suggesting the letter "may have been a prank" (p. 9).
- ONI Serial 38-49 detailed Shell's 27 May 1949 sighting of 6-7 oval discs in file formation at 190-260 MPH along Hart Mountain, Oregon (p. 26, p. 28).
- The ONI officer ruled out conventional aircraft, balloons, astronomical phenomena, vertigo, and canopy reflections (p. 30).
- ONI document was later declassified by NIS letter 9/28/77 for researcher Bruce S. Maccabee (p. 26).
