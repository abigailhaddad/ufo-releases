# DOW-UAP-D10, Mission Report, Middle East, May 2022

**Record:** 35 · **Agency:** Department of War · **Incident:** 5/6/22, Iraq
**Source:** `data/text/35.txt` · **Pages:** 6

## Summary
This is a USCENTCOM MISREP from Operation INHERENT RESOLVE, originated by an AFCENT ISR asset (432 AEW) flying an XCAS/REC mission with FMV and SIGINT primary sensors (p. 2, p. 5). Between takeoff at 0246Z and landing at 0004Z the next day, the aircraft conducted target development on a redacted objective and recorded one formal UAP observation entry (p. 1, p. 4, p. 5). The Observation gentext states that from 1514Z to 1934Z the operator "observed 5X UAP fly across the screen," with the first at 1514Z resembling a "possible [redacted] missile" and the four subsequent UAPs fitting "closer to the profile of possible birds" (p. 6). Weather is noted as dust that "hindered most FMV collection of the ground" (p. 6). The document was declassified by MG Richard A. Harrison, USCENTCOM Chief of Staff, on 7 October 2025 and approved for release to AARO (p. 1).
