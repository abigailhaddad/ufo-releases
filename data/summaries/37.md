# DOW-UAP-D14, Mission Report, Iraq, May 2022

**Record:** 37 · **Agency:** Department of War · **Incident:** 5/29/22, Syria
**Source:** `data/text/37.txt` · **Pages:** 9

## Summary
This SECRET//REL TO USA, FVEY mission report (MISREP) documents a 20-hour 30-minute ISR sortie launched from Sigonella Airbase (LICZ) in Sicily on 29 May 2022 in support of the 603 AOC and Operation HUMMER SICKLE over the Eastern Mediterranean (p. 1, p. 4, p. 5). During target development on Syrian Navy operating areas and OPIR Israel-Lebanon-Syria region targets, the aircraft collected on 39 targets including naval bases, coastal defense sites, and airfields (p. 6). At 0117Z on 30 May, the aircraft observed one possible small UAP and followed it as long as possible; the screener could not get a positive ID (p. 1, p. 8). Earlier, at 2147Z, the asset was reacted to by 1-3 aircraft including one possible Russian Aerospace Forces SU-30 that came within approximately 5NM but did not impact the mission (p. 1, p. 8). The document was declassified by MG Richard A. Harrison, USCENTCOM Chief of Staff, on 8 October 2025 (p. 1).

## Key facts
- Takeoff from LICZ (Sigonella) at 291355Z MAY 22; landed LICZ at 301025Z MAY 22 (p. 4).
- Mission type ISR under ATO E149, originator 50 ATKS, 432 AEW (p. 2).
- On station in SRO track 41EMD at 1551Z; off station at 0616Z, total 14h 25m on station (p. 5).
- Collected SIGINT via AIRHANDLER from 1507Z to 0906Z (p. 1).
- Conducted IMINT on 39 targets including Tartus Naval Base, Latakia Naval HQ, Bassel al-Assad Intl Afld, and Beirut military facilities (p. 6).
- Observed Russian naval movements: Slava CG hull 055, Udaloy I DDs hulls 626 and 564, Gorshkov FFG hull 461 (p. 6).
- Observed 1x A-50U Mainstay D and 2x IL-38 parked, plus probable IL-76 and SU-27/35 activity at a Syrian airfield (p. 6).
- Reaction event: possible RFAF SU-30 flew under aircraft orbit (FL190 vs FL243) for ~2 minutes before turning away (p. 8).
- UAP initial contact 300117Z MAY 22; friendly aircraft at FL240, 142 KIAS, location 36SXD9 (p. 8).
- One UAP sighted, no signatures recorded, flew north to northeast (p. 8).
