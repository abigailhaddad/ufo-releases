                                and repeating for 3 or 4 such passes. By thiE time all of the objects had
                                disappeared. I expended the balance of the film late that afternoon on a
                                mountain somewhere in Idaho.

                  L Analysis of the graph correlating luminosity and size
          indicate that as the luminosity of the objects increase, the size

                     '
                   It is felt that if these images are reflective surfaces,
          blinking would occur during a period of 90 seconds while passing
          through a n angle of E,o degrees when photographed at the rate of
          16 frames per second. This premise would permit highly reflective
          ball oons or spheroids to be a possibility . However, the velocit :,, and
          acceleration computations (sub paragraph 8) rule this possibility out.
          It appears, therefore, that if these objects are a reflecting surface,

          and acceleration attributed to the objects may be caused by light
          reflected off a highly refl ective surface onto a n atomospheric screen

          of the sun angle and atmospheric condition reported at the time of
          photography and because of the variations in luminosity which, under
          the above conditions would remain constant.

     a. Light Source. The opinion that these images are
light sources is based on the time they can be viewed
continuously on the film, approximately 90 seconds, and
on the a.Dgle through which they were photographed, approx­

reflected light, blinking would occur during a period of
90 seconds while passing through an angle of 60 degrees
when photographed at the rate of 16 frames per second.
Although this premise would permit aircraft and balloons
to be the source, they are ruled out because of the velocity
determination. If aircraft is assumed to be close enough
to permit velocities of present aircraft, they would be
close enough for positive identification. If they are too
far away for positive identification, then the velocity is
too great for any aircraft as we know them.
