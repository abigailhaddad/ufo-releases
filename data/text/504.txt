Talking Paper for the Director

SUBJECT: (U) Senator Reid Visit to Director, DIA, 05 January 2009

1. (U) PURPOSE: To provide LTG Maples with the current status of the Advanced Aerospace
Weapon System Applications contract should Senator Reid raise the topic.

2. (U/;lf@WO) BACKGROUND: Senator Inouye (D-HI) and Senator Reid (D-Nev) sponsored
language in the July 2008 Supplemental Appropriation bill for DIA to investigate foreign
advanced aerospace weapon threats from the present out to 40 years in the future. Senator Reid
has a deep interest in the potential threat posed by unconventional aerospace technologies; that
is, technologies that are a radical departure to those used in current advanced aerospace vehicles.
In response to this tasking, the Acquisition Support Division (DW0-3) published a request for
proposals (RFP) on 18 August 2008. DIA received only one bid, from Bigelow Aerospace
Advanced Space Studies (BAASS), in response to the RFP. DIA/ AE and DIA/DWO determined
that the proposal was responsive and the contract signed on 22 September 2008. Since the
Director's meeting with Senator Reid on 13 November 2008, the following items have been
completed per the contract.
